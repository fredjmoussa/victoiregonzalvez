Victoire Gonzalvez (b. 1997) is an artist based in Paris. Her practice intertwines installation, text, and performance, slitting open interstitial spaces inhabited by personal and collective anecdotes, missteps, and spillover.

She studied at the École des Arts Décoratifs (Paris) and was a Weissenhof Fellow (2022–23) at the Staatliche Akademie der Bildenden Künste Stuttgart.

She is a 2–12 residency fellow for 2026 at the Cité internationale des arts in Paris.
