Alternative text:
There are always pieces of bread stuck inside the benches. The plastic squeaks with every movement. I wish I could stretch further because everything keeps overflowing. À force de prier et de vivre d’espoir, to clear the fog. Sometimes I really wonder what I am looking for. Café- cigarette on the quay at Ruisbroek, a flash on your shirt at first sight, a straight pause in short sleeves letting the air circulate along your back. Blue scales on a dry curve. A bottle of sangria shaped like a penis................................ Nobody comes to bother you when you are a tourist. The businessmen, the businessseurs, who come to have fun, get laid and leave. Some of them are completely obsessed with sex, stuck and convinced they have to go and see prostitutes. Desire is present as an internal charge, not yet addressed, not shared. Two bodies share a space without direct relation. A secret of confectioners who are hungry. The fruit is immersed in increasingly concentrated syrups until saturation. The sugar colonizes. A fourth stomach, taken after slaughter. Rennet sacrifice... The sun has risen tearing through the fog. I will be able to sing, cry, scream. Maybe all these things are connected by the fact that they were never meant to stay together. A scan, a song, a manga, a body, a supermarket, a city at the border, a sentence overheard in a hairdresser’s, something caught in the upholstery of a bench.

Victoire Gonzalvez (b. 1997) is an artist based in Paris. Her practice intertwines installation, text, and performance, slitting open interstitial spaces inhabited by personal and collective anecdotes, missteps, and spillover. Through sampling found materials and adapting analog special effects know-how – such as life casting and flesh modeling – she explores the bonds between imitation and control, with a focus on sexual dissidence and affective responses to power.

She studied at the École des Arts Décoratifs (Paris) and was a Weissenhof Fellow (2022–23) at the Staatliche Akademie der Bildenden Künste Stuttgart alongside performance group Discoteca Flaming Star, where she taught workshops as a guest lecturer (2023–24).

Her work has been shown in solo and group exhibitions, as well as residencies in France, Germany, and Belgium. She has also collaborated on prosthetics for films, exhibitions, and performances in France and Italy.

She is a 2–12 residency fellow for 2026 at the Cité internationale des arts in Paris.
