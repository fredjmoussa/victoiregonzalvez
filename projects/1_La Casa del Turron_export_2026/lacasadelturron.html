La Casa del Turrón (2026)

An installation project blending fiction, materials, and writing workshops.

Written at the beginning of the residency at Villa Belleville, La Casa del Turrón is a short story resulting from documentary research on the commercial activities of Els Limits, a town on the French-Spanish border, and its culture of erotic clubs, shaped by mass tourism from France to Spain.

In these spaces, “non-passage” takes shape through surfaces designed to be easily cleaned and to erase all traces of use: linoleum floors, washable plastics, tightly stretched sheets, and materials that return to their original state after each use.

Starting from this absence of traces, the project reflects on the intimacy surrounding the clients and their activities, as well as on the presences that remain hidden within these spaces.

Four leather tapestries form the installation, covered with a patina made from grease and pigments developed during the residency. Together, they recreate a fictional club space.

Within this space, collective writing workshops explored erotic fiction through the experience of “overflow.” Through text and movement, the workshops explored how overflow can disrupt closed or cyclical situations.
